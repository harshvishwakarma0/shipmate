    Here's what you should know
    before handing over the keys.

    Is this another AI tool?

    No. Shipmate is a service powered by a real, dedicated builder. They use AI tools where helpful, but a person makes the decisions, tests the work, and stays accountable to the agreed scope.

    Which tools can you work with?

    Lovable, Replit, Cursor, and Claude Code are our starting point. Your builder works in your existing project and accounts. We'll confirm compatibility before you commit.

    What if my project is a complete mess?

    That's a good reason to start with a Rescue audit. We review what works, what's broken, and what it would take to ship. If a rebuild or specialist is the better option, we'll say so.

    What does “one active project” mean?

    You can keep a list of ideas, but your builder focuses on one agreed project at a time. Once it's finished or paused, you choose what comes next, within your monthly building time.

    Can I pause my subscription?

    Yes. You can pause between projects without losing the work or project context. We'll agree on the pause date, remaining work, and resumption details before the next billing period.

    Who owns the code and accounts?

    You do. Work happens in accounts you control, with access you can revoke. You keep the code, assets, and handover. We never deploy to production without your approval.

    What if the work needs a specialist?

    We identify the limit rather than guessing. Your builder prepares a scoped handover with what's built, the remaining decisions, and relevant code. Specialist implementation is quoted separately.

    Does a security review guarantee a secure app?

    No review can guarantee that an app has no vulnerabilities. Our checklist covers common launch risks. Regulated data, complex permissions, and high-risk systems may need an independent security specialist.
